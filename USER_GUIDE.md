# User Guide — Vidya Setu Impact Dashboard

**For Non-Technical NGO Staff**

*This guide will help you navigate the Impact Dashboard, understand what each chart means, and use the data to make better programme decisions.*

---

## Getting Started

### Opening the Dashboard

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended)
2. Go to the dashboard URL provided by your IT team
3. The **Executive Summary** page loads automatically

### What You See First

The dashboard has two main sections:

- **Left sidebar** — Navigation menu to switch between different views
- **Main area** — Charts, numbers, and tables showing your programme data

---

## Understanding the Sidebar Navigation

| Menu Item | What It Shows | When to Use |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Executive Summary** | Overall programme health at a glance | Your **Monday morning** check-in |
| **KPI Tree** | How activities lead to outcomes | **Monthly reviews** and board presentations |
| **Programme Progress** | Target vs actual for each programme | **Weekly** programme management |
| **Demographic Reach** | Who we're reaching (gender, age, location) | **Quarterly** equity reviews |
| **Cost-per-Impact** | How efficiently we're using funds | **Budget planning** and donor reports |
| **Trend Analysis** | How things are changing over time | **Strategic planning** meetings |
| **Data Quality** | How reliable our data is | **Operations** reviews |

---

## Using Filters

At the top of every page, you'll see three dropdown filters:

### Programme Filter
- **All Programmes** — Shows combined data across all 4 programmes
- Select a specific programme (e.g., "After-School Tutoring") to focus on it

### Region Filter
- **All Regions** — Shows all 5 regions (South, North, West, East, Central)
- Select a specific region to see only that area's data

### Period Filter
- **All Time** — Full 18-month dataset
- **Last 6 Months** / **Last 3 Months** / **Last Month** — Narrow the time window

**Tip:** Filters apply to ALL charts on the current page. They reset when you switch pages.

## Page-by-Page Guide

### 1. Executive Summary

This is your **homepage**. It answers: *"How is the programme doing overall?"*

#### The Monday Number (Impact Score)
- The big number at the top is your **Composite Impact Score** (out of 100)
- It combines: learning gains (60%), retention (20%), and programme reach (20%)
- **Goal:** Keep this above 50. Below 30 needs immediate attention.

#### KPI Cards (the 4 coloured boxes)
| Card | What It Means | Good Sign | Warning Sign |
|------|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Active Beneficiaries** | How many people are currently in our programmes | 85%+ retention | Below 70% retention |
| **Total Sessions** | How many classes/workshops we've conducted | On track with targets | Below 50% of target |
| **Avg. Reading Gain** | How much reading scores improved | +5 points or more | Flat or negative |
| **Cost per Beneficiary** | How much we spend per person | Decreasing over time | Rising rapidly |

#### Charts
- **Monthly Trend** — The area chart shows sessions (purple) and participants (green) over time. Both should be trending up or stable.
- **Programme Distribution** — The donut chart shows how our sessions are split across programmes.
- **Activity by Region** — The bar chart shows which regions are most active.

---

### 2. KPI Tree

This page shows **how our work creates impact**, step by step:

```
Activity (what we do) 
  → Output (what we produce) 
    → Outcome (what changes) 
      → Impact (why it matters)
```

**How to read it:**
- **Activity row:** Sessions held, hours invested, districts covered
- **Output row:** People trained, sessions completed
- **Outcome row:** Test score improvements, retention rates
- **Impact row:** Cost-effectiveness, overall improvement rates

**Use this for:** Board presentations, donor meetings, and understanding your Theory of Change.

---

### 3. Programme Progress

Shows **target vs actual** for each of the 4 programmes.

- **Progress bars** — Green means on track (>80%), yellow means watch (50-80%), red means behind (<50%)
- **Participants** — Total people reached by each programme
- **Total Cost** — Money spent on each programme
- **Monthly Stacked Bar Chart** — Shows how sessions are distributed across months and programmes

**Action items:**
- If a programme is below 50%, investigate why (staffing? logistics? demand?)
- Compare cost vs participants to see efficiency

---

### 4. Demographic Reach

Shows **who we're reaching** — critical for equity monitoring.

- **Gender Distribution** — Pie chart showing male/female/other split
- **Age Distribution** — Bar chart of age groups
- **Category-wise Outcomes** — Radar chart comparing reading/math/digital gains across General, OBC, SC, ST categories
- **District Table** — Performance metrics for each district

**Key questions to ask:**
- Are we reaching enough women/girls? (Target: >50%)
- Are SC/ST beneficiaries getting equal outcomes?
- Which districts need more attention?

---

### 5. Cost-per-Impact

The **finance view** — shows how efficiently we're using money.

- **Total Investment** — Overall spending
- **Cost per Active Beneficiary** — What we spend per person who stays in the programme
- **Most Efficient Programme** — Which programme gives best value
- **By Funding Source** — Where our money comes from
- **Efficiency Table** — Detailed breakdown of cost/session, cost/participant, cost/outcome

**For donor reports:** Focus on the Cost-per-Outcome column — this is what funders care about most.

---

### 6. Trend Analysis

Interactive view with **4 tabs** to explore time-series data:

| Tab | What It Shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **Sessions & Participation** | Monthly sessions, participants, quality scores |
| **Cumulative Growth** | Total growth over the entire project period |
| **By Programme** | How each programme is trending independently |
| **By Region** | Regional activity patterns over time |

**Look for:**
- Upward trends (good!) or downward trends (investigate)
- Seasonal patterns (e.g., drops during exam seasons)
- Regional disparities

---

### 7. Data Quality

Shows how **reliable and complete** our field data is.

- **Quality Score** — Percentage of records without issues (target: >90%)
- **Verification Rate** — What proportion of records have been verified
- **Issue Types** — Most common data problems
- **Field Worker Scorecard** — Individual performance of each field worker

**Action items:**
- Workers with quality score below 80%: schedule training
- Common issues (e.g., "missing_attendance"): update the data collection form
- Low verification rate: allocate more time for data review

## Recommended Check-in Cadence

| When | What to Check | Time Needed |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Every Monday** | Executive Summary → Impact Score + KPI Cards | 5 minutes |
| **Every Week** | Programme Progress → Are we on track? | 10 minutes |
| **Every 2 Weeks** | Data Quality → Any problems to fix? | 10 minutes |
| **Monthly** | Full review: Demographics + Trends + Costs | 30 minutes |
| **Quarterly** | KPI Tree + Cost Analysis → Board presentation prep | 1 hour |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Dashboard is loading slowly | Check your internet connection. Close other browser tabs. |
| Charts look empty | Try changing the filters to "All Programmes" and "All Time" |
| Numbers don't match my spreadsheet | The dashboard uses cleaned data. Check the Data Quality page for flagged records. |
| I can't see my district | Make sure the Region filter is set to "All Regions" |

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — a number that tells us how well we're doing |
| **Beneficiary** | A person who participates in our programmes |
| **Retention Rate** | The percentage of enrolled people who are still active |
| **Cost-per-Impact** | How much money it takes to achieve one unit of measurable change |
| **Data Quality Score** | How clean and complete our field data is |
| **Baseline Score** | A beneficiary's score BEFORE they joined our programme |
| **M&E** | Monitoring & Evaluation — the practice of tracking and assessing programme performance |
| **Theory of Change** | The logic model showing how our activities lead to impact |
